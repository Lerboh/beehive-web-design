A programme designed for growth.

BeeHive uses a four-phase partner structure. Businesses decide what progression, rewards and opportunities make sense within their programme. The phases belong to the platform, not to one side of it.

  1. 01 · Start

    Begin the relationship.

  2. 02 · Active

    Participate, promote and start building results.

  3. 03 · Grow

    Unlock broader opportunities and deeper collaboration.

  4. 04 · Professional

    Develop a structured commercial partnership.

One identity. Different partnerships.

The same person can work with more than one business through BeeHive. Progress, rules, rewards and opportunities stay with each business.

Business

Your programme. Your rules.

These areas describe what the platform is being built to support. They are not a claim that every function is already live.

  • Performance

    Designed to support progress and performance visibility inside each business programme.

  • Rewards

    Rewards a business defines for its own partners. Amounts and conditions stay with that business.

  • Settings

    The settings a business uses to run its programme in its own way.

Partner

Grow with the businesses you believe in.

  • Campaign opportunities

    Campaigns are designed to stay with the business that created them.

  • Reward visibility

    Rewards are designed to be visible per partnership, under that business’s rules.

  • Professional growth path

    The four phases are a professional route. Each business decides what the later phases mean.
