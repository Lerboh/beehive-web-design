BeeHive platform

A programme designed for growth.

BeeHive uses a four-phase partner structure. Businesses decide what progression, rewards and opportunities make sense within their programme.

  1. 01 · Start

    Begin the relationship.

  2. 02 · Active

    Participate, promote and start building results.

  3. 03 · Grow

    Unlock broader opportunities and deeper collaboration.

  4. 04 · Professional

    Develop a structured commercial partnership.

Business

Your programme. Your rules.

A business configures its own programme inside BeeHive. These areas describe what the platform is being built to support. They are not a claim that every function is already live.

  • Programme structure

    BeeHive is being built to support a structure a business can shape: phases, requirements and what opens up as a partnership develops.

  • Partner management

    Designed to support an organised view of the people connected to one business, kept apart from every other programme.

  • Campaigns

    Designed to support campaigns a business can offer to the partners in its own programme.

  • Progress & performance

    Designed to support progress and performance visibility inside each business programme.

  • Rewards

    Designed to support rewards a business defines for its own partners. Amounts and conditions stay with that business.

  • Business settings

    Designed to support the settings a business uses to run its programme in its own way.

Partner

Grow with the businesses you believe in.

  • One BeeHive identity

    One profile for the person. Each partnership stays attached to the business it belongs to.

  • Multiple business relationships

    The same BeeHive identity can connect with more than one participating business.

  • Clear progress

    Each partnership is designed to show its own progress, separate from the others.

  • Campaign opportunities

    Campaigns are designed to stay with the business that created them.

  • Reward visibility

    Rewards are designed to be visible per partnership, under that business’s rules.

  • Professional growth path

    The four phases are a professional route. Each business decides what the later phases mean in its programme.
