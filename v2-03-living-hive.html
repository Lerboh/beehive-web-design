Rules

Business

Your programme. Your rules.

A business configures its own programme inside BeeHive. These areas describe what the platform is being built to support. They are not a claim that every function is already live.

  • Programme structure

    Phases, requirements and what opens up as a partnership develops.

  • Partner management

    The people connected to one business, kept apart from every other programme.

  • Campaigns

    Campaigns a business can offer to the partners in its own programme.

  • Performance, rewards, settings

    Visibility, rewards and settings stay with that business. Amounts are not invented here.

Partner

Grow with the businesses you believe in.

Work with participating businesses through one BeeHive identity while each partnership keeps its own progress, rewards and opportunities.

  • One BeeHive identity

    One profile for the person. Each partnership stays with its business.

  • Multiple relationships

    The same identity can connect with more than one participating business.

  • Progress, campaigns, rewards

    Each stays visible per partnership, under that business’s rules.

  • Professional growth path

    Four phases. Each business decides what the later phases mean.

BeeHive platform

A programme designed for growth.

BeeHive uses a four-phase partner structure. Businesses decide what progression, rewards and opportunities make sense within their programme.

  1. 01

    Start

    Begin the relationship.

  2. 02

    Active

    Participate, promote and start building results.

  3. 03

    Grow

    Unlock broader opportunities and deeper collaboration.

  4. 04

    Professional

    Develop a structured commercial partnership.

BeeHive platform
