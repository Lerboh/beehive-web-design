For businesses

Build a partner programme around your business.

BeeHive gives your business one structured programme for bringing partners in, deciding what they may promote, and developing the relationship over four phases.

The problem

Partnerships get scattered.

Affiliate codes, ambassadors, creators, referrals, campaigns and rewards often live in different spreadsheets and message threads. BeeHive is a partner-programme platform. Its value is structure: one programme for your business, with clear rules for who joins and what they may do.

What you control

The business sets the terms.

  • Programme rules

    Eligibility, conditions and what “progress” means for your business.

  • Who can join

    You decide which partners belong in this programme.

  • The offer

    Which products or services partners are allowed to promote.

  • Campaigns

    Seasonal work, launches and content assignments you choose to run.

  • Rewards

    Commission, benefits and recognition stay yours to define.

  • Requirements

    Content standards and business-specific conditions.

Four phases

A framework you configure.

The four phases are fixed as a structure. They are not BeeHive subscriptions, and BeeHive does not set your sales thresholds. You decide what each phase asks for and what it offers.

  1. 01 · Onboard and evaluate

    A partner enters your programme and you see whether the relationship should continue.

  2. 02 · Activate and motivate

    Partners take part, promote within your rules, and start building the relationship.

  3. 03 · Deepen the commercial relationship

    Broader opportunities open when you decide the partnership is ready.

  4. 04 · A professional partnership

    A more structured commercial collaboration, still on your terms.

Your partner pool

One business. Its own programme.

Your partners, phases, rewards and progress belong to your programme. A partner may also work with another business. That relationship stays separate. It does not merge into yours.

Planned capability

Campaigns the business organises.

BeeHive is being designed so a business can offer specific work inside its programme: a product, a season, a launch, or a content assignment. Partners only promote what that business has approved.

  • Products and services

    The offer partners are allowed to talk about.

  • A defined campaign

    A launch, a season, or a promotion with a clear brief.

  • Your conditions

    Timing, materials and what “done” means stay with you.

Questions from businesses

What is BeeHive?

BeeHive connects a business with partners who help that business grow. The business runs a programme. The partner keeps one identity and can join more than one business, separately.

Can we decide our own rewards?

Yes. Commission, benefits and other rewards are set by the business, not by a BeeHive price list.

Can we choose how partners progress?

Yes. The four-phase framework is fixed. The criteria and benefits inside it are yours.

Are the four phases fixed?

The sequence is the BeeHive framework: onboard, activate, deepen, then a professional partnership. BeeHive does not invent a sales threshold for you.

Can partners work with other businesses?

Yes. Phase, progress, terms and rewards stay separate for each business.

Does our programme stay separate?

Yes. Another company’s partner relationship is not part of your programme.

Can we decide which products partners promote?

Yes. Partners promote the products and services you approve for your programme.

How does setup work?

The business joins, the programme is configured, the offer and rules are defined, then partners can be accepted into that programme. The working product is still in development.

Are prices final?

No. Setup and the ongoing plan are still to be defined.
