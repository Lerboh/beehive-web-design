Step 01 · Business

The business creates its programme.

You choose the offer, the phases and what partners are allowed to promote.

A business opens a programme. Your BeeHive identity is not that business.

Step 02 · Partner

A partner joins.

The partner keeps one identity and a separate relationship with you.

You join this programme without merging it with any other business.

Step 03 · Campaign

The campaign begins.

The campaign uses the products and rules you set.

You receive the materials for this business only.

Step 04 · Promotion

The partner creates the promotion.

Filming, demonstrating or explaining the product is the work of promotion.

Step 05 · Customer

A customer engages.

The product is in the customer’s hands. Nothing here is a claimed sales result.

Step 06 · Progress

Progress returns to the programme.

What happened stays visible inside your programme.

Your progress with this business does not transfer to another.

Step 07 · Next phase

The phase indicator can move.

Start, Active, Grow, Professional. The business decides the criteria. BeeHive does not invent a sales threshold.

Step 08 · Another business

The same partner can join a different business.

Company B is a placeholder. The relationship, phase and rewards stay separate from the first programme.
