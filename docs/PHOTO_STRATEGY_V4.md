# Photo strategy V4

Eleven new photographs. Neutral products, no external logos, no BeeHive mark printed into the scene. People are visual stand-ins: no names, quotes or results.

## business/business-choose-offer.webp

ROLE: Business

ACTION: Selecting a jar from shelves of jars and plain boxes.

CONTEXT: A stocked shop, inventory visible.

USED IN: 09 Kinetic Hive (business node and business section), 11 Cinematic Commerce (chapter 1)

WHY IT SUPPORTS BEEHIVE: Shows the business choosing what a partner programme can offer, with the stock in frame.
