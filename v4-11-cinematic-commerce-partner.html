The role

You join a programme. You do not become the business.

A BeeHive partner promotes products and services a business has approved, takes part in that business’s work, and can grow inside the rules that business set. You can do this with more than one business. Each relationship keeps its own phase, progress and rewards.

Four phases

Growth inside a business’s rules.

Phases are not a promise of income. The business decides what each phase requires. BeeHive does not publish a sales threshold.

  1. 01 · Start

    Join and learn how that business wants the relationship to work.

  2. 02 · Active

    Participate, promote and start building results inside the rules that business set.

  3. 03 · Grow

    Broader opportunities open when the business says the partnership is ready.

  4. 04 · Professional

    A deeper commercial relationship, still specific to that business.

The kind of work a partnership can include.

These are the activities BeeHive is being shaped around. A business chooses which of them apply. They are not all available as finished tools today.

  • Share a referral

    A code or link the business provides.

  • Promote approved products

    Only what that business has allowed.

  • Create content

    Film, photograph or explain the offer.

  • Join a campaign

    A launch, a season, or a brief from the business.

  • Meet customers

    Recommend the product in a real conversation.

  • Stay with the relationship

    Events and longer commercial collaboration, when the business offers them.

Questions from partners

What is a BeeHive partner?

A person with one BeeHive identity who can join the programmes of participating businesses.

Can I work with more than one business?

Yes. Each business keeps its own phase, rules, opportunities and rewards.

Is my phase the same everywhere?

No. Phase belongs to the relationship with that business.

Who decides my rewards?

The business. BeeHive does not set a universal commission.

Do I have to buy the products?

BeeHive does not require that. A business may set its own conditions for its programme.

Do I manage shipping?

BeeHive is not the shop. Promoting a product does not make you responsible for that business’s orders or shipping.

Can I choose what to promote?

You promote what that business has approved. You do not add your own products to its programme.

How do I progress?

By the criteria that business configured. There is no BeeHive sales threshold.

Can I leave a business programme?

Ending one programme is designed to leave your BeeHive identity, and your other partnerships, in place.

What happens to my profile?

The identity stays yours. Partnerships stay separate from each other.
