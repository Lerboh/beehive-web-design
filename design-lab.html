BeeHive Visual Design Lab

Four visual directions for review

V1 concepts are archived and are not part of this comparison. Baseline V1 stays unchanged.

01

Human Network

People and a photo honeycomb. Businesses and partners are two photographic panels joined by a gold BeeHive cell.

Palette. Cream, deep forest, honey, gold.

Photography. Portraits cropped into the hive, plus full-bleed panels.

Business. Forest overlay and a deep green programme band.

Partner. Warm ivory panel and a honey chapter.

Hexagons. Dense hero cluster, large fragments behind later sections.

Open concept

02

Split Stories

Two stories at once. A diagonal split hero, then business, partner and shared chapters in turn.

Palette. Forest green against honey cream, gold only for BeeHive.

Photography. Full-height environmental portraits on each side.

Business. Deep green chapters, honey icons.

Partner. Warm chapters, forest icons.

Hexagons. Large outlines crossing the chapter breaks, not a wallpaper.

Open concept

03

Living Hive

The honeycomb is the page. Photos, words, icons and empty cells sit in one field, and the field changes as you scroll.

Palette. Stone cream, forest cells, honey cells, a gold centre.

Photography. Almost only inside cells, mixed with open cells.

Business. A green cluster and a forest chapter.

Partner. A warm cluster and honey chapter.

Hexagons. Structural. Density is high in the hero and looser in the process.

Open concept

04

Premium Editorial

Magazine scale. A large serif headline, one dominant photograph, and hexagons laid over the image.

Palette. Ivory, charcoal, forest, gold, a small clay accent.

Photography. Large rectangular frames, with one hex crop on the hero.

Business. A full forest band, image beside a feature list.

Partner. An ivory editorial split and a short list.

Hexagons. A few very large shapes, not a field of small outlines.

Open concept
