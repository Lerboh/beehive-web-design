Business

The product starts with the business.

A business prepares what partners can promote, and sets the programme those partners move through. BeeHive is being built to keep that programme with the business.

BeeHive

BeeHive is the layer between them.

The same person can work with more than one business. Progress, rules, rewards and opportunities stay with each business.

Partner

Partners turn the product into promotion.

Work with participating businesses through one BeeHive identity. Each partnership keeps its own progress, campaigns and rewards.

How the movement works

  1. 01

    Business creates its programme

    The business decides phases, requirements and what opens up.

  2. 02

    Partners join and promote

    Partners connect and create activity around that business.

  3. 03

    Customers meet the product

    Promotion is meant to reach a real customer, not stop at a link.

  4. 04

    Progress unlocks opportunity

    Growth stays inside the programme the business defined.

A programme designed for growth.

BeeHive uses a four-phase partner structure. Businesses decide what progression, rewards and opportunities make sense. These pictures are stages of involvement, not named users.

  1. 01 · Start

    Begin the relationship.

  2. 02 · Active

    Participate, promote and start building results.

  3. 03 · Grow

    Unlock broader opportunities and deeper collaboration.

  4. 04 · Professional

    Develop a structured commercial partnership.
