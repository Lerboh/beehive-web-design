Platform

BeeHive platform

Built to hold a programme, not a pile of links.

These areas describe what the platform is being built to support. They are not a claim that every function is already live.

  • Programme

    Phases and requirements for one business.

  • Partners

    People connected to that business only.

  • Campaigns

    Offers that stay with the business.

  • Performance

    Progress visible inside the programme.

  • Rewards

    Defined by the business. Not priced here.

Partner

Grow with the businesses you believe in.

One BeeHive identity can connect with more than one participating business. Each partnership keeps its own progress, campaigns and rewards.

Business

Your programme. Your rules.

A business configures its own programme inside BeeHive. Partners, campaigns and rewards for that business stay apart from every other programme.
